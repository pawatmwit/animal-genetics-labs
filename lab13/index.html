Lab Worksheet 13 · Trees from DNA

710 114 Animal Genetics

Before you begin · ก่อนเริ่ม

Compare short DNA sequences from chickens, junglefowl and cattle, build family trees from the differences, and look for a sign of selection in a genome. Answers save in this browser, so use the same device for the whole session.

About the data. The sequences are 40 bases long and were designed for this class. They follow the pattern found in real studies (domestic chickens closest to the red junglefowl subspecies spadiceus; cattle in taurine and zebu groups), but they are not real sequences.
