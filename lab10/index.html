Part A · Instruments · ไมโครปิเปต เครื่องชั่ง เครื่องปั่นเหวี่ยง

A1 · How accurate is your pipetting?

Tare a weighing boat on the balance, pipette distilled water into it, and record the mass shown in grams. Re-tare between readings. Water at room temperature: 1 µL ≈ 1 mg, so 0.200 g ≈ 200 µL. Use the analytical balance (0.0001 g) for the P20.

PipetteSet toRep 1 (g)Rep 2 (g)Rep 3 (g)Mean (µL)AccuracyCV

Aim for accuracy 97–103% and CV ≤ 2%. Outside that, check your technique (first stop, angle, slow release) and repeat.

A2 · Balance a 12-place centrifuge rotor

Tap slots to load tubes (all tubes hold the same volume). The rotor is balanced when the tubes' centre of mass is at the middle.
