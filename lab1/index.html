Part B · Chromosome parts and centromere types

A chromosome at metaphase (เมทาเฟส) has already copied its DNA, so it is made of two identical sister chromatids joined at the centromere.

B1 · Name the numbered parts

B2 · Classify by centromere position

Metacentric (เมทาเซนทริก): centromere in the middle. Submetacentric (ซับเมทาเซนทริก): off-centre. Acrocentric (อะโครเซนทริก): very near one end. Telocentric (เทโลเซนทริก): at the end.

Part C · Cattle karyotype · แคริโอไทป์โค

The dark circle is a metaphase spread from one animal, seen under the microscope. Drag each chromosome into the karyogram below. Put homologous chromosomes (โครโมโซมคู่เหมือน) side by side: same length, same centromere position, same band pattern. Order the pairs from largest (1) to smallest (29). Put the sex chromosomes in the last box.

  1. First, count every chromosome in the spread and write 2n below. Tap a chromosome to mark it while counting if you like.
  2. Drag, or tap a chromosome and then tap a box, to place it.
  3. Press Check karyotype at any time. Green boxes are correct pairs; red boxes need another look.

Part D · Chicken karyotype · แคริโอไทป์ไก่

Chickens have a few large macrochromosomes and many tiny microchromosomes (ไมโครโครโมโซม) that are too small to pair by eye. Drag the 22 larger chromosomes into pairs 1–10 and the sex chromosome box. Then count the microchromosomes: tap each tiny dot to mark it as counted.
