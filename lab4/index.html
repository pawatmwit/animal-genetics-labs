Part A · Curly calf in Angus cattle · พันธุประวัติ 1

Curly calf syndrome (arthrogryposis multiplex) causes calves to be born dead with twisted limbs. Shaded = affected calf.

Part B · Polled cattle · พันธุประวัติ 2

Shaded = polled (born without horns); open = horned.

Part C · Haemophilia A in dogs · พันธุประวัติ 3

Affected dogs bleed for a long time after small injuries because a clotting factor is missing. Shaded = affected.
