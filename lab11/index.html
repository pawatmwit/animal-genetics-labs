Part C · Run BLAST on NCBI · ใช้ BLAST กับข้อมูลจริง

Steps

  1. Open NCBI Gene and search MSTN Bos taurus. Open the cattle myostatin gene.
  2. Find the gene's mRNA reference sequence (its accession starts with NM_) and open it. Record the accession and its length below.
  3. On that record, choose Run BLAST (or copy the sequence in FASTA format and paste it into Nucleotide BLAST). Search the standard nucleotide database.
  4. When the results appear, look past the cattle hits. Record the first three other species and their percent identity.
  5. Try to find a pig (Sus scrofa) hit further down and record its percent identity.

NCBI changes its pages from time to time; if a button has moved, ask your instructor.
