Rules you will use

TATA box: TATAAA, about 25–30 bp before the transcription start.

Start codon: the first ATG after the transcription start.

Introns: start GTAAGT or GTGAGT; end with a run of C and T, then AG.

Poly-A signal: AATAAA; the RNA is cut 10–30 nt after it.

Part B · From gene to mRNA, step by step · จากยีนสู่ mRNA ทีละขั้น

A cell turns the gene into mature mRNA in three steps. Press the buttons in order and watch the sequence change, then answer the questions.

Step 1 · Cut out the introns

The exons you found in Part A are kept and joined; the introns are removed. In RNA, T becomes U.

Step 2 · Add the cap and the tail

A cap is added to the 5′ end and a long poly-A tail to the 3′ end. They protect the mRNA and help ribosomes find it.

Step 3 · Find the start codon and read in threes

The ribosome starts at the first AUG and reads three bases at a time until a stop codon. Grey bases before AUG are the 5′ UTR.

Part C · Cattle MSTN in Ensembl · ยีนไมโอสแตตินของโค

Myostatin (MSTN) is the gene behind double muscling in Belgian Blue cattle and heavy muscling in Texel sheep.

1. Open ensembl.org.

2. Choose Cow as the species and search for MSTN; open the gene page.

3. Read the location (chromosome), the transcript table, and the protein length of the main transcript.

If Ensembl shows something different from what the checker expects, write what you see in the note box and tell the instructor.
