Rules you will use

TATA box: TATAAA, about 25–30 bp before the transcription start.

Start codon: the first ATG after the transcription start.

Introns: start GTAAGT or GTGAGT; end with a run of C and T, then AG.

Poly-A signal: AATAAA; the RNA is cut 10–30 nt after it.

Part D · Cattle MSTN in Ensembl · ยีนไมโอสแตตินของโค

Myostatin (MSTN) is the gene behind double muscling in Belgian Blue cattle and heavy muscling in Texel sheep.

1. Open ensembl.org.

2. Choose Cow as the species and search for MSTN; open the gene page.

3. Read the location (chromosome), the transcript table, and the protein length of the main transcript.

If Ensembl shows something different from what the checker expects, write what you see in the note box and tell the instructor.
