Lab Worksheet 14 · Be the breeder

710 114 Animal Genetics

Before you begin · ก่อนเริ่ม

You run a small chicken breeding flock. Each generation you choose 4 cockerels and 10 hens as parents from 40 birds; the simulator breeds them and shows the next generation. Then you check the breeder's equation, R = h² × S, against what happened.

How the simulator works

Each bird has genes (a breeding value) and an environment effect; you see only the total, its record (P = G + E). Offspring get half of each parent's breeding value plus a random share, as in real meiosis. Every run is different, just like a real flock. Your flocks are saved in this browser.

Part A · Meet the flock · รู้จักฝูงไก่

Generation 0 of the broiler flock from Part B: body weight at 6 weeks (kg). Each dot is a bird; cockerels on top, hens below. Hover or tap a dot for its weight.

Part B · Select for body weight · h² = 0.4

Choose the heaviest birds as parents for 3 generations. After each choice, work out S and predicted R; after breeding, work out the observed R.

Part C · Select for hatchability on records · h² = 0.05

Hatchability (% of a hen's eggs that hatch; for cockerels, the % from their test matings). Choose the best birds on their records for 3 generations.

Part D · Same trait, genomic values · การคัดเลือกจีโนม

A new flock with the same trait. Each bird now also has a genomic breeding value (GEBV) from a SNP chip. Dots are placed by GEBV; choose the birds with the best GEBV.

Part E · Random parents · กลุ่มควบคุม

A new broiler flock. This time pick parents at random (use the button) for 2 generations.

Part F · Explain · อธิบาย

Write 2–4 sentences for each, in English or Thai, using your own results.
