Lab Worksheet 2 · Virtual beads and chi-square

710 114 Animal Genetics

Before you begin · ก่อนเริ่ม

You will breed virtual cattle by drawing beads from parent bags, count the calves, and test the counts with a chi-square test. You do the chi-square by hand with a calculator; the worksheet checks each step. Answers save in this browser, so use the same device for the whole session.

E = black coat, e = red coat (MC1R gene). P = polled, p = horned (POLLED gene). Every draw is random, so your counts will differ from your classmates'.

Part A · Probability warm-up · ความน่าจะเป็น

Type each answer as a fraction (for example 3/16) or a decimal (0.1875). Then check your answers.

Each parent is a black carrier (Ee). Its bag holds equal numbers of E and e beads. Each calf gets one bead from the sire's bag and one from the dam's bag. Breed 100 calves.
