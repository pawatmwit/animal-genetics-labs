Lab Worksheet 12 · Populations in a computer

710 114 Animal Genetics

Before you begin · ก่อนเริ่ม

Calculate frequencies for real-looking herds, then run three population-genetics models from Virtual Biology Lab (East Tennessee State University) and record what happens. Answers save in this browser, so use the same device for the whole session.

The three models (open each in a new tab)

1. PopGen Fish Pond: Hardy–Weinberg conditions, one at a time

2. Random Genetic Drift (directions): Buri's fly vials

3. Random Genetic Effects (directions): bottlenecks and founders

Use a laptop in landscape: the models need a large screen. Keep this worksheet in its own tab.

Part A · Herd calculations · คำนวณจากข้อมูลฝูง

The herds are invented but realistic. Round to the decimals asked.

Part B · PopGen Fish Pond · บ่อปลาคาร์ป

Open PopGen Fish Pond. Koi colour is set by one gene with two alleles; heterozygotes look mottled. Its directions are built into the model: use the model's own labels for population size, fitness, migration and mutation. Record the proportion of one allele (pick one and stick with it).

B1 · Equilibrium (all conditions met)

Use a large population, equal fitness, no mutation or migration. Run about 50 generations, three times.

RunStartAfter ~50 gen.

B2 · Selection

Lower the fitness of the fish homozygous for your chosen allele; keep everything else as in B1. Run three times.

RunStartAfter ~50 gen.

B3 · Migration

Reset fitness to equal. Turn on migration with immigrants rich in the other allele. Run three times.

RunStartAfter ~50 gen.

Part C · Random Genetic Drift · การทดลองของ Buri

Open Random Genetic Drift. Ten vials of flies; the bw allele (brown eyes) is tracked. Switch Stop_When_Fixed on, set Init_Prop_bw and Pop_Size, click Reset, then Go. Read Mean Fix. Time and each vial's Prop. bw at the end.

Part D · Random Genetic Effects · คอขวดและผู้ก่อตั้ง

Open Random Genetic Effects. Three alleles: Yellow, Blue, Red. Click Setup, then Go, and let the mainland settle (about 30 generations) before each step. Record the allele proportions shown.

PopulationYellowBlueRedSum

Mainland: default Mainland-K. Bottleneck: set Bottleneck-Size to 10, click Bottleneck, run 20 generations. Island 1: Colonize-1 with Founding-N-1 = 5 and Isle-1-K = 200. Island 2: Colonize-2 from Island 1 with Founding-N-2 = 5.

Part E · Explain · อธิบาย

Write 2–4 sentences for each, in English or Thai, using your own results.
